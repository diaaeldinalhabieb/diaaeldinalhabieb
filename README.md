👋 About :
I approach data analysis as an investigative and decision-support discipline, not simply as a process of writing queries and producing dashboards.

My work starts with a fundamental question:

What are we trying to understand, what evidence would answer it, and what can the data legitimately support?

My analytical approach follows:

Question → Validation → Evidence → Analysis → Finding → Insight → Decision

My professional background in legal investigation strengthened my approach to evidence assessment, source verification, structured reasoning, uncertainty, and analytical documentation. I now apply that mindset to data and business analytics.

🧠 How I Think
Question Before Query

A technically correct analysis can still be useless if it answers the wrong question.

I therefore begin by defining:

The business problem
The analytical question
The relevant population or entity
The dimensions and measures required
The stakeholder or decision the analysis is intended to support
Evidence Before Interpretation

I do not treat a query result as an insight, a pattern as causality, or an assumption as a fact.

I distinguish between:

Observation → Validated Finding → Insight → Business Interpretation → Recommendation

If the evidence is insufficient, the conclusion remains a hypothesis or observation rather than being presented as a fact.

Validation Before Trust

Before interpreting data, I examine whether the underlying structure can support the question.

This includes considering:

Data quality
Analytical grain
Relationships and join logic
Missingness and duplication
Temporal structure
Aggregation risks
Structural limitations

Schema before assumptions.
Grain before aggregation.
Evidence before interpretation.

⏱️ Temporal & Longitudinal Thinking

For time-dependent analysis, I explicitly consider:

Invented relationships
Unsupported joins
Logic drift
Unsupported findings
Causal overreach
Narrative inflation

For SQL-related work, AI assistance should remain bounded by the verified physical schema and available evidence.

AI can accelerate analysis. It cannot replace analytical judgment.

🛡️ Reproducibility & Analytical Governance

I value analytical work that can be understood, reviewed, and reproduced by someone other than its author.

Where appropriate, analytical artifacts preserve:

Analysis / investigation identity
Purpose
Tables and fields used
Join logic
Analytical grain
Temporal anchor
Duplicate-prevention logic
Evidence lineage
Output and status
Documentation

The principle is simple:

If a conclusion matters, its path back to the evidence should be traceable.

📊 Data Quality & Validation

Data quality is treated as part of the analytical problem, not merely as a final checklist.

Depending on the question, validation may address:

Missing keys
Null-heavy fields
Duplicate records
Logical inconsistencies
Referential limitations
Invalid mappings
Join-induced inflation
Many-to-many relationships
Temporal inconsistencies
Historical overwrite risks
Aggregation distortion

If a structural issue can materially affect the conclusion, it must be investigated before the conclusion is accepted.
